Punjab

Punjab is a state in northern India. Its capital is Chandigarh, which is also the capital of Haryana. Punjab is famous for its agriculture, traditional Punjabi culture, delicious food, music, and festivals. The Golden Temple in Amritsar is one of the most famous religious and historical places in India. Ludhiana, Jalandhar, and Patiala are important cities.
