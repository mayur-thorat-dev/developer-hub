About Paris

Paris is the capital city of France and one of the most beautiful tourist destinations in the world. It is famous for its historical monuments, beautiful architecture, museums, fashion, food and romantic atmosphere. Millions of tourists visit Paris every year to experience its culture and famous attractions.

Famous Attractions in Paris

1. Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is the most famous landmark of Paris. Visitors can enjoy amazing views of the entire city from the top of the tower.

2. Louvre Museum

The Louvre Museum is one of the world's largest art museums. It is famous for its glass pyramid and Mona Lisa painting.

3. Arc de Triomphe

Arc de Triomphe is a famous historical monument in Paris. It represents French history and honors those who fought for France.
